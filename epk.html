// Biography

The Story

Doc 01

"Clutch by nature, classic by design."

Calvin Priice is a multi-genre recording artist from The Bronx, New York, whose music lives at the intersection of faith, love, and real life. A New York native with Dominican and Puerto Rican roots, he writes and records in both English and Spanish — moving between genres and languages the way he actually talks.

His sound doesn't fit in one box, and that's exactly the point. Across a growing catalog of singles, his music reflects personal growth, spiritual depth, and the kind of honesty that makes people feel less alone. Independent and self-driven, Calvin builds his world on his own terms — from the studio to the streets.
