About

Index 03 — The story

Clutch by nature, classic by design.

New York native with Dominican and Puerto Rican roots, Calvin Priice — often searched as Calvin Price — is a multi-genre recording artist whose music lives at the intersection of faith, love, and real life. His sound doesn't fit in one box, and that's exactly the point.

His music reflects personal growth, spiritual depth, and the kind of honesty that makes people feel less alone.

Frequently Asked

Index 05
01
Is Calvin Priice the same as Calvin Price?

Yes. Calvin Priice is the official spelling of the artist's name — the double "i" is intentional. He's often searched as "Calvin Price," but his music, social profiles, and this official site all use the spelling Calvin Priice.

02
Where is Calvin Priice from?

Calvin Priice is from The Bronx, New York, with Dominican and Puerto Rican roots.

03
What kind of music does Calvin Priice make?

Multi-genre — pop, R&B, hip-hop, funk, and Latin/EDM — built around themes of faith, love, and real life. Listen to his latest music.

04
Where can I stream Calvin Priice?

On Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and most major platforms.
