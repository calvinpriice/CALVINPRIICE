The Story

"Faith over everything."

New York native with Dominican and Puerto Rican roots, Calvin Priice is a multi-genre recording artist whose music lives at the intersection of faith, love, and real life. His sound doesn't fit in one box — and that's exactly the point.

His music reflects personal growth, spiritual depth, and the kind of honesty that makes people feel less alone.
