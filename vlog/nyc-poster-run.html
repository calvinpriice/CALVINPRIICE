NYC Poster Run

Part 1 of 4. The posters went up all over New York: 14th Street, Harlem, the West Side Highway, the Lower East Side, Alphabet City. The best part happened off camera.

Guerrilla marketing, done by hand. The episode opens on the posters going up, then cuts to the ride home and the story of the day: people stopping to look, asking if it's me, scanning the QR code and listening right there on the sidewalk. A fan from South Africa asked for a poster, then asked me to sign it. That was the first signed Calvin Priice poster.

Social media can make you overly performative, chasing how things look and what drops on what date. Being outside, meeting people and having real conversations is the other half of the job. To close it out I swing back by one of the walls next to the L train, still up days later, before heading to Little Italy for the Feast of San Gennaro.
