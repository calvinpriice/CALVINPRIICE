Festival Eats & the Flight Home

Part 4 of 4. Eating our way through San Gennaro, then the AirTrain, the flight, and landing back in Los Angeles.

A piña colada, a stop at Skinny Joey's cheesesteak stand where the crew is working nonstop all festival, and a run-in with a Twitch streamer on the block. Then empanadas: cheese pizza and shredded chicken, with a homemade cherry lemonade. (The mic was in my pocket for part of this, so bear with the audio.)

Then the trip wraps the way they all do: the AirTrain, an overnight flight, and "welcome to Los Angeles." That's New York and San Gennaro, start to finish.
