Carnival Games at San Gennaro

Part 3 of 4. The carnival games at San Gennaro. Three balls for twenty dollars, and a game operator who has seen every trick.

Every festival has one game everybody thinks they can beat. This one is a basketball toss where the ball has to stay in, and the rim doesn't count. The crew takes turns, the operator runs the rules like a coach, and new friends get pulled in from the crowd.

Strategy, trash talk, some real near misses, and a running tab that climbs past eighty dollars. Nobody's leaving with the big prize, so the next move is obvious: food.
